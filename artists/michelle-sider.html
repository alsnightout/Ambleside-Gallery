THE ARTIST

Where light
and emotion
converge.

Michelle Sider is a Michigan-based mixed media artist recognized for her richly detailed mosaics and paintings. Her artistic practice explores universal themes of identity, place, spirituality, and light.

Her work is often described as ethereal and dramatic, distinguished by bold, imaginative color and a compelling sense of movement, texture, and flow.

Through a painterly approach to mosaic and mixed media, Sider creates compositions that combine intricate detail with expressive visual depth.

Her work is intended to evoke strong emotions, inviting viewers to experience the relationship between material, color, and personal interpretation.

Whether working with glass, smalti, acrylic, or other materials, Sider approaches each composition as an opportunity to explore the expressive possibilities of light and texture.

THE PRACTICE

A language
of color,
texture and light.

Michelle Sider's artistic practice is rooted in the expressive possibilities of mixed media and mosaic. Through the careful arrangement of materials, she develops compositions that balance intricate detail with dramatic visual movement.

Stained glass, smalti, and other richly textured materials allow light to interact with the surfaces of her work, creating depth and visual complexity.

Her imaginative use of color transforms familiar subjects into expressive compositions, encouraging viewers to look beyond representation and consider the emotional qualities of an image.

Themes of spirituality, identity, and place remain central to her work, connecting her choice of materials with a broader exploration of human experience.

THE ARTIST

Where light
becomes
atmosphere.

In Deborah Squier's landscapes, the natural world is rendered through an intimate exploration of light, color and atmosphere.

Her paintings often dissolve the boundaries between earth and sky, allowing subtle transitions of tone to suggest distance, movement and the passage of time.

Working in pastel and oil on linen, Squier creates compositions that balance the recognizable landscape with a deeply atmospheric visual language.

Rather than relying on sharply defined detail, the paintings invite contemplation through softened edges, nuanced color and the quiet presence of light.

THE WORK

The quiet
language
of nature.

Across Squier's paintings, familiar elements of the landscape become studies in atmosphere and perception.

Woodlands, distant hills, shifting clouds and open horizons emerge through delicate relationships between color and light.

Her works invite a slower kind of looking, encouraging the viewer to notice the subtle beauty of changing weather, fleeting illumination and the natural world.

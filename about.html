About the Gallery

For more than forty years, Ambleside Gallery has united exceptional global artistry with meaningful local connection.

AMBLESIDE

A legacy of art.
A place for connection.

For more than forty years, Ambleside Gallery has brought exceptional artists and collectors together through a shared belief in the transformative power of fine art.

Our collection spans representational and contemporary abstract work by distinguished painters, sculptors and photographers from around the world.

More than a place to view art, Ambleside is a creative sanctuary — an inviting environment for discovery, reflection and conversation, and a bridge between the artists who give form to their vision and the collectors who welcome those stories into their lives.

OUR HISTORY

From England
to North Carolina.

1982

Manchester, England

Ambleside Fine Arts was founded in Manchester, England by Jackson Mayshark.

1994

Grosse Pointe, Michigan

Ambleside moved to Grosse Pointe, where it established its first brick-and-mortar gallery and showcased its distinctive collection of British art.

1994–2003

A Growing Collection

During the following years, the gallery welcomed new artists, presented more than fifty exhibitions, hosted special events and became increasingly involved in its artistic community.

2003

North Carolina

Ambleside Gallery relocated to North Carolina, beginning another chapter in the gallery's history.

THE NEXT CHAPTER

Where every brushstroke tells a story.

Building upon nearly four decades of artistic excellence, Ambleside continues to discover extraordinary artists, present exceptional work and cultivate meaningful relationships between artists, collectors and the community.

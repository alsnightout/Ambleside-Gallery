MUSIC AT AMBLESIDE

Art is meant
to be
experienced.

Music at Ambleside brings live performance into the gallery for intimate gatherings surrounded by works of art.

The program is envisioned as an extension of the gallery itself — a place where visual art, music and conversation can exist together in an atmosphere that is personal, thoughtful and engaging.

From singer-songwriters and instrumentalists to pianists and small ensembles, performances at Ambleside offer artists and audiences an opportunity to experience music within the distinctive setting of the gallery.

THE EXPERIENCE

An intimate
room for
music.

Music at Ambleside is designed around closeness rather than spectacle.

Guests gather within the gallery among paintings, sculpture and photography, creating an environment in which the performer and audience share the same space.

With a piano housed within the gallery, Ambleside welcomes the possibility of performances ranging from solo piano and voice to acoustic music and other intimate arrangements suited to the space.

Upcoming performances, admission details and reservation information will be announced here as events are scheduled.

FOR MUSICIANS

Perform at Ambleside

Ambleside welcomes inquiries from musicians interested in performing as part of our developing music program.

We are especially interested in performances suited to the intimacy of the gallery — artists whose music encourages listening, connection and an experience complementary to the visual art surrounding it.

Musicians interested in being considered for a future performance are invited to introduce themselves and share examples of their work.

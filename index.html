OUR STORY

About the Gallery

Established in England in 1982, Ambleside Gallery has developed a reputation for presenting exceptional fine art to collectors from near and far.

The gallery brings together artists whose work reflects remarkable craftsmanship, originality, integrity and artistic depth.

THE ARTISTS

Artists of skill and distinction.

Ambleside represents painters, sculptors and photographers whose work demonstrates exceptional artistic ability and a distinctive point of view.
